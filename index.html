Startup: AI Lecture Assistant

AI Lecture Assistant aims to help university students understand and study their lectures more effectively. The application uses AI to transcribe and translate lectures, generate structured notes and summaries, and create flashcards for revision. Teachers can review and edit the generated materials, providing students with a reliable study reference.
